Having logins in your wite is a big responsibility Adobe, 2013 — 153 million accounts. The passwords were encrypted, not hashed, which produces identical output for identical input. So every user with the password 123456 had the same ciphertext. The dump also included every user’s plaintext password hint. Sources: Schneier, “Cryptographic Blunders Revealed by Adobe’s Password Leak”, CSO Online: encrypted, not hashed, Have I Been Pwned record, XKCD 1286.
